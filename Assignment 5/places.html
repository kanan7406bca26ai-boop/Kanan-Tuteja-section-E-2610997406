Famous Places in India

1. Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is a beautiful monument in Agra and is one of the most famous places in India.

2. India Gate

India Gate is a famous war memorial located in New Delhi.

3. Red Fort

The Red Fort is a historic fort in Delhi built during the Mughal period.

4. Gateway of India

The Gateway of India is a famous monument situated in Mumbai.

5. Hawa Mahal

Hawa Mahal is a beautiful historical palace located in Jaipur, Rajasthan.
